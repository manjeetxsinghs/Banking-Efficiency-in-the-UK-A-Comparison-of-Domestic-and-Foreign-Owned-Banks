# **Banking Efficiency in the UK: A Comparison of Domestic and Foreign-Owned Banks**

## **Overview**

This project evaluates and compares the operating efficiency of six major UK banks (Lloyds, NatWest, Nationwide, HSBC, Barclays UK, and Santander UK) across the financial years 2022 to 2025\. Using Data Envelopment Analysis (DEA), the study models 24 Decision Making Units (DMUs) under the intermediation approach to assess how effectively banks convert resources into lending and financial outcomes.

## **Key Features**

* **Multi-Model DEA Implementation:** Utilises Constant Returns to Scale (CRS/CCR), Variable Returns to Scale (BCC/VRS), Scale Efficiency, and Super-Efficiency models.  
* **Output-Oriented Analysis:** Focuses on output expansion factors for net loans and total revenue relative to total deposits and operating expenses.  
* **Statistical Testing:** Implements the Mann-Whitney U Test to examine whether performance differences between domestically-owned and foreign-owned banks are statistically significant.  
* **Outlier Identification:** Identifies structural outliers using super-efficiency scores and outlier thresholds.

## **Methodology & Variables**

* **Inputs (Minimised):** Total Deposits (£m), Operating Expenses before credit impairment (£m).  
* **Outputs (Maximised):** Net Loans (£m), Total Revenue (£m).
